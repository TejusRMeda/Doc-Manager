FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free! No account required, no hidden fees, no premium tiers. We built this to help travelers organize their visa applications without the stress.

Do you store my documents?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your documents never leave your device unless you choose to download the merged PDF.

Which countries are supported?

We have templates for all 27 Schengen countries. You can also create custom lists for any destination.

Can I use this on my phone?

Absolutely! The tool is fully responsive and works great on phones and tablets. Perfect for checking off items on the go.

How accurate are the document requirements?

Our templates are based on official VFS and embassy websites. However, requirements can change, so always verify with the official source before your appointment.

What file formats can I upload?

You can upload PDFs, JPEGs, PNGs, and most common image formats. We'll handle the conversion and merging for you.
