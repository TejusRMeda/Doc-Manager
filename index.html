How It Works

Four simple steps to organized documents

1

Add your travelers

Create a tab for yourself, then add tabs for your partner, kids, or anyone else applying together. Each person gets their own independent checklist.

2

Upload your documents

Go through the checklist and upload each document — passport scan, bank statements, insurance certificate, flight booking, hotel confirmations. PDF or images both work.

3

Reorder if needed

Drag documents to match your consulate's preferred order. Mark any documents you've already printed separately so they're excluded from the export.

4

Export and print

Click Export to generate a single PDF with all documents, section dividers, and a cover page. Take it to any print shop, print, and you're done.
